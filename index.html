About

Student Finance Tracker is a simple expense tracker for students. Record your spending, track it against a budget cap, and find transactions with regular-expression search. Your data is saved in your browser and is not uploaded.

Built with semantic HTML, responsive CSS, and modular vanilla JavaScript. No frameworks.
